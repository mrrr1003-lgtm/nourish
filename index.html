How photo logging works

Experimental: an open-source vision model runs on your device. It compares your image with food names and suggests possible matches. The first run downloads model files, which may take a while and use significant data.

Choose the correct food, then enter its weight or portion. Calories and macros are calculated from nutrition data, not measured from the image.

⚠️ Photo estimates are never exact, especially for oils, sauces, mixed dishes and portion sizes. Check the suggested food and adjust grams before saving.
🔒 Your photos stay on your device for analysis; Nourish doesn't upload them to a photo-analysis server; Google Lens is a separate service and uploading there is optional. Your diary is saved in this browser only.

Your data & privacy
